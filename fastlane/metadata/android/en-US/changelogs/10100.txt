The 3D book: read a real-looking book in a place, with a cover that opens, page edges that grow as you read and pages that curl under your finger. Eight places, each drawn or as a photo, moving and with their own sound made live: a desk, a library, by the fire, mountains, a park, a rainy window, a beach at sunset and a starry night.
